Job title: Senior Backend Engineer (Distributed Systems)

About the role
We are building a high-throughput payments platform and are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to design, build and operate distributed services that process millions of events per day.

Responsibilities
- Design and own scalable, fault-tolerant backend services using Node.js or Go and TypeScript.
- Build event-driven systems with Kafka and manage caching layers with Redis.
- Design PostgreSQL schemas and optimise queries for high-volume workloads.
- Deploy and operate services on Kubernetes and AWS; improve observability with metrics, tracing and alerting.
- Define API standards, rate limiting and idempotency patterns; review designs and mentor engineers.
- Lead incident response and drive reliability improvements (SLOs, retries, circuit breakers).

Requirements
- 4+ years of backend engineering experience.
- Strong knowledge of distributed systems concepts: consistency, partitioning, replication, queues.
- Hands-on experience with Kafka, Redis, PostgreSQL and Kubernetes.
- Experience with CI/CD, Docker and cloud infrastructure (AWS preferred).
- Strong system design and communication skills.

Nice to have
- Experience with gRPC, GraphQL or Go.
- Open-source contributions.
